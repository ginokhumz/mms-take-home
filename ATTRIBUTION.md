## What is derived

Two exercises in this pack are adaptations of design material from that work.

1. The greenfield problem in `01-greenfield-design/` adapts a timeline and search design exercise.
   The scale figures, the added requirements and the deliverable structure are ours and differ
   from the original.
2. The existing design in `02-critique-and-extend/EXISTING-DESIGN.md` adapts a snippet-sharing
   design. It is rewritten in our own words, the names are fictional, and the diagram is redrawn.

## Changes made

- All company, brand and product names are replaced with fictional or generic names.
- All capacity figures are changed.
- New requirements are added that the original does not carry.
- The prose is rewritten. No diagram, image or code file from the original is reproduced here.
